About Barça Database

Barça Database is an independent FC Barcelona statistics and records project designed for supporters, researchers and football enthusiasts. The database brings together historical match data, player appearances, goals, assists, managerial records and opponent statistics in a searchable format.

Whether you want to explore Barcelona legends, compare players from different eras, analyse competition performances or review historical seasons, Barça Database provides detailed information covering more than a century of FC Barcelona history.
